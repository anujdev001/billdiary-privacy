🔒 Privacy By Architecture

Privacy Policy for BillDiary

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.0

Welcome to BillDiary. We believe your financial records, purchase receipts, warranties, and subscription commitments are deeply personal. Our application is engineered from the ground up to be offline-first — your data lives securely on your device, not on our servers.

Our Core Privacy Guarantee: BillDiary does not operate private user tracking servers. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest your personal bills, receipts, invoices, or bank/payment data.

1. Architecture & Local Data Storage

BillDiary operates on an offline-first architectural model:

  • Encrypted Local Database: All entries you add (merchant names, purchase dates, amounts, categories, warranty expiration dates, and subscription costs) are saved in an encrypted local database (Hive) residing strictly on your device's private sandbox storage.
  • Receipt Photos & Invoices: Any photo you take with your camera or select from your gallery is saved directly to your device's private app storage.
  • 100% On-Device OCR Recognition: Optical Character Recognition (OCR) for extracting receipt text is performed entirely on your device using Google ML Kit Vision. Receipt images are never uploaded to any cloud server for scanning or parsing.
  • Biometric Security: If you enable App Lock (Fingerprint, Touch ID, or Face ID), authentication is performed solely by your mobile device's secure hardware enclave (Android BiometricPrompt / iOS LocalAuthentication). The app never sees, accesses, or stores your biometric markers.

3. Third-Party Services & Diagnostics

To deliver high stability, seamless cloud backup, and reliable in-app upgrades, BillDiary incorporates minimal, industry-standard diagnostic and platform SDKs:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): Gathers anonymized telemetry on general app usage. No personal billing records or receipt details are ever transmitted.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): Captures unexpected exceptions, device model, and OS version to enable rapid bug fixes.
  • Google Drive API (Optional Backup): When you enable cloud backup, BillDiary creates an AES-encrypted archive directly in your own personal Google Drive app data folder. We do not operate intermediate servers, and BillDiary has zero access to any of your other Google Drive files.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit: Digital purchase transactions are handled end-to-end by Google or Apple. BillDiary never receives or stores your credit card numbers or billing details.

4. Device Permissions

BillDiary only requests permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver user-initiated features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively to take pictures of paper receipts and warranty documents for on-device OCR scanning.
  • Photo Library / Media Images: Allows you to pick existing receipt photos or PDFs from your gallery.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Enables scheduled local alarm notifications to alert you before upcoming subscription renewals or expiring warranties.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): Enables biometric hardware authentication for instant, secure vault unlock.

5. User Data Deletion & Retention Policy

Because BillDiary does not retain your personal bills on developer servers, you have immediate, sovereign control over all your data at all times:

How to Delete Your Data:

  • Individual Items: Swipe or tap delete on any bill, receipt image, or subscription to permanently remove it from your device.
  • Complete In-App Wipe: Open Settings > Data Management to clear all local databases and purge cached images in one tap.
  • Cloud Backup Deletion: If you created a Google Drive backup, you can delete the backup file directly from within the app settings or via your Google Drive storage management.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling BillDiary automatically and irreversibly deletes all local vault databases and receipt photos from your phone.

Note: Because all records are stored strictly on your local device or your own private Google Drive, all deletions are 100% instant, autonomous, and self-managed without needing server-side requests.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

BillDiary is a financial and warranty organization tool intended for adults and young adults (ages 18 and older). It is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security Standards

All network communications between the app and third-party APIs (Google OAuth, Sentry, Firebase) are strictly transmitted over industry-standard TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 (HTTPS) encryption. Local backup archives are protected with AES encryption.
