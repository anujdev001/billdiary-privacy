Privacy Policy for BillDiary

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to BillDiary. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you about how we handle your data when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Our primary goal is to provide you with a seamless, secure, and bug-free experience. We access and process the following information:

  • Google Account Information: We use Google Sign-In to securely authenticate you. We receive your basic profile information (name and email).
  • App Data & Bills: Information regarding your bills and tracker data that you explicitly enter into the app. This is stored securely on your local device.
  • Camera & Photo Library: We require access to your camera and photos to scan receipts. This image processing (OCR) is done entirely on your device using Google ML Kit. Images are not uploaded to our servers.
Important Note on Google Drive: If you use our cloud backup feature, BillDiary requests access to your Google Drive. We do not read, view, or analyze your files. We only use this permission to safely save your encrypted BillDiary backup file to your own personal Google Drive so you never lose your data.

2. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Crash Reporting

To ensure the app runs smoothly, we use industry-standard third-party services that may collect anonymous usage data:

  • Firebase Analytics: We use Google Firebase to collect anonymous data on how users interact with the app. This helps us understand what features to improve.
  • Sentry (Crashlytics): If the app crashes, Sentry automatically collects anonymous diagnostic data (like your device model and OS version) to help us find and fix the bug immediately. No personal bill data is sent in these reports.
  • In-App Purchases: Payments and subscriptions are processed securely by Google Play Billing or Apple App Store. We do not have access to your credit card information.

3. Data Security & Biometrics

We take your privacy seriously. If you enable App Lock (Biometrics/Face ID/Fingerprint), all authentication happens locally on your device's secure hardware. We do not have access to your biometric data. Furthermore, we do not sell your personal data to any third parties.
